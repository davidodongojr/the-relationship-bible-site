Every day

Today’s Reflection

Five minutes with Jesus before the day gets loud.

Each morning there’s one reflection waiting, picked for your season of life. Read it, sit with the Scripture, and tap Reflect when you want to talk it through.

  • Matched to whether you’re single, dating or married
  • 21 days with Jesus lights up as you go
  • A grace day each week, so a missed day isn’t a fail
For couples

Closer

A game for two, a card at a time.

You both answer the same small question about yourself, then guess your person’s answer. Nothing shows until you’ve both played, then the two flip over together.

  • For dating and married couples
  • Answers stay sealed until you’ve both played
  • Some cards end with a little “Now I get you”
Together

Plans you read with your people

Same plan, everyone at their own pace.

Start a plan and invite up to ten people with a link: your spouse, your small group, a friend. You’ll see who has read each day. What you write stays yours.

  • Guided plans for building, strengthening and repairing
  • Invite with a link, no searching for people
  • Your notes are never shared
Bible & Play

Read it. Then swipe it.

Ten true-or-false questions, about two minutes.

Read the Bible in your translation, highlight and keep notes. Then see what stuck with today’s Bible Swipe: right for true, left for false.

  • A fresh round every day
  • Categories from Old Testament to parables
  • Practice mode with no timer

Take a minute, or stay a while.

  • Calm, Pray and Find strength
  • One minute or a few
  • Real people one tap away if you’re ever in danger
