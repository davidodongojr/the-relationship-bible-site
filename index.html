Private, careful, human

Your inner life is not engagement material.

Your reading, notes, highlights, reflections, and personal journey are not turned into a public profile or shown to church leaders. There is no social feed and no username directory.

Private by design

Personal reading and reflection remain personal. Support Circle connections require an exact username shared directly by someone you know.

Honest about limits

The reflection companion is not a pastor, counselor, emergency service, or substitute for qualified professional care.

A path to real people

When language suggests a crisis, normal reflection stops and the app brings forward ways to connect with trusted or professional human support.
