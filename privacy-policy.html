The Relationship Bible

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Who we are

The Relationship Bible (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Christ-centered relationship discipleship app operated under the name The Relationship Bible, Kampala, Uganda. For privacy questions or requests, contact therelationshipbibleapp@gmail.com.

This policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS and Android apps, their home-screen widgets, and their related web pages.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and profile information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the name and email address that service shares with us. Our authentication provider stores a protected form of any password; we do not see or store it in plain text. We also generate an editable username so people who already know it can request to add you to their Support Circle.

You may optionally provide your date of birth, gender, marital status, denomination, and one or more life seasons. We derive your age from your date of birth for personalization. You can edit this information in the App.

We store the colour theme and Light / Dark / Follow system choice you make in the App, on your device and on your profile, so we can count how many readers choose each one. Staff see only totals, never who chose what.

2.2 Reading, learning, and content you create

We store Bible references you save or open, highlights, notes, Expression and Journal documents, lesson encounters, the days you read, plan enrollments and progress, Library reading positions and book highlights, Relationship Map attention, and similar journey activity needed to provide and personalize the App. Reflection Rhythm and the optional 21 days with Jesus are calculated from these reading days.

Bible verse text is fetched through our Bible service and is not stored in our database. Chapters may be cached on your device so previously opened Scripture remains available and loads quickly. If you copy or share a verse, or export a Scripture picture, that happens on your device and you choose where it goes.

Expressions may optionally let you choose a photo from your device as a background. If you do, we upload that photo to private, account-scoped storage so you can reopen the Expression. We do not access photos you do not select. Your Expression document stores the private image reference and design position, not another copy of the image or any Bible wording.

2.3 Companion conversations

When you choose to use Companion, we collect the messages you send and the generated replies. We also send the model provider contextual information needed to respond appropriately. Depending on what you have provided and where you opened Companion, this can include:

  • the current lesson, plan, Bible passage, or topic;
  • your life seasons, derived age, gender, and denomination;
  • lessons and plans explored or completed, dimension progress, active-plan context, and aggregated Bible Swipe performance; and
  • recent messages in the current conversation.

We do not send your name, email address, username, raw date of birth, Support Circle membership, private notes, or highlights to the model provider. Later turns use a reduced context, but recent conversation messages remain necessary to continue the exchange.

We store Companion conversations in our database for operation, safety, reporting, and review. By default the App starts each visible Companion session fresh. If you switch on "Keep my conversations", the App shows your conversation from the moment you switched it on, as one continuous thread, and sends the most recent messages in that thread back to the model provider as context for your next message. Switching it off, or off and on again, empties the chat box: earlier messages are no longer shown or sent as context. Switching it off does not delete messages already stored for the purposes above. The App also shows how many Companion messages you have left, from a count of messages used that contains none of their content.

2.4 Support Circle and support directory

We store Support Circle requests and accepted account links. There is no public username directory or username search; someone must know your exact username and you must accept their request. Either person can remove the connection.

The professional-support directory is readable in the App. If you contact a provider, you leave the App and communicate through the provider’s phone number, website, email, or booking service. We do not send the provider your profile or Companion data.

2.5 Doing things with other people

Some features show a little of your activity to people you have chosen:

  • Plans together. When you start or join a plan with others, the group sees who has read which day. Nothing you write is shared.
  • 7 days together. The two people see only whether the other read each day.
  • Closer (the couples game). Your answers and guesses are shared with the one person you play with, and each card stays sealed until you have both played. Choosing Stop playing together deletes the pair's game data for both of you.

Invitations to these features can travel as links. On Android, if you install the App from such a link, Google Play passes the link to the App so the invitation is waiting when you open it.

2.6 Bible Swipe gameplay

We store game sessions, answers, categories, XP, streak, badges, and personal-best information. Bible Swipe performance stays within Play except for the Scripture-streak invitation described in the App, and aggregated performance may be used as a Companion journey signal. If you join a church room or game group in Play, its organisers see who has joined and overall results for its rounds.

2.7 Christian Living, Trusted Finds, and ministries

Christian Living may store private care plans, saved recommendations, practice and check-in records, and Relationship Map landmarks so they remain available across your devices.

Location for Finds is an optional country and city you type on the Me tab. It is used only to show Hangouts, Churches and Events near you, and is never obtained from device location services. Trusted Finds open the retailer's or provider's own page in your browser; nothing is bought in the App.

If you suggest a Find, we store what you submitted and your account so staff can review it. A published suggestion is labelled "Suggested by a reader", never with your name. If you report a Find, we store the Find, your reason, any detail you add, and the review status.

If you follow a ministry and choose Notify me, we store that choice so its new publications reach your Inbox and, where you allow notifications, your device. Scanning a ministry's code uses the camera only while the scanner is open; no picture is kept. Ministries choose their own profile picture in the church portal; staff approve it before it appears.

2.8 Moments and the Relationship Assistant

When you choose to use Moments, we store the information you deliberately enter about people and situations you want to care for. This may include a person's display name and relationship label, dates and occasions, preferences or things they mentioned, commitments, your desired outcome, preparation and follow-up actions, completion status, and reminder intent.

Birthdays from your contacts or calendar. In Moments you can choose Add → From your contacts or From your calendar. Only then does the App ask your phone's permission. It reads names and birthdays (contacts) or birthday events (calendar) on your device to show you a list, and nothing is sent anywhere while you choose. Only the people you tick are added, as a name and a birthday, and then stored like anything else you enter in Moments. Phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, other contact details and other calendar events are never stored or uploaded. The App never changes your contacts or calendar. You can turn either permission off at any time in your phone's settings.

Moments information is private to your account and is encrypted on your device before it is saved there. It synchronizes to owner-scoped database records so it can survive reinstall or appear on another signed-in device. Reminder notifications are scheduled by the device and use generic lock-screen wording that never includes a person's name or your own wording.

When you deliberately submit text to the Relationship Assistant, we send that submitted text to our server and Anthropic's commercial API to interpret it into a structured proposal. We do not attach your existing Moments people, memories, actions, contacts, calendar, email, profile, Companion history, or other private relationship information. The proposal is not saved until you review, correct, and confirm it. We store a content-free daily usage count to enforce the feature limit; it records usage, not what you wrote.

2.9 Notifications

Daily reminders, Moments reminders, and gentle reminders after a few days away are scheduled on your device; they are not sent from our servers. Permission is requested only when you turn on a reminder or choose a time.

Push notifications come from our servers only because someone just did something that concerns you, such as inviting you to a plan, joining your plan, playing a Closer card, or a ministry you follow publishing something. To send them we store your device's push token with your account. A push never shows a name, a plan title, or anything anyone wrote. Signing out removes the device's token; turning off From your people stops these pushes.

2.10 Feedback

If you send feedback from the App, we store your message, its kind (idea, problem or thanks), and your account so we can reply or follow up. If you choose to attach a screenshot, it is uploaded to private storage. To help us look into a problem, we also store the App version, the platform, the operating system version, and the phone model.

2.11 Home-screen widgets

If you add one of our widgets to your home screen, the App saves today's verse, the title of today's reflection, and which days this week you read, on your device only, so the widget can show them. This is removed when you sign out.

2.12 Diagnostics and updates

We use Sentry to receive crash reports, performance traces, and diagnostics such as app version, device model, operating system, timestamps, stack traces, screen or route context, and network information such as an IP address where the service receives it. We do not intentionally attach Companion messages, notes, highlights, Moments information, passwords, or Bible selections to Sentry reports.

The App checks with the App Store or Google Play, and with our servers, whether a newer version is available, and downloads small updates from Expo's update service. These checks send ordinary technical information, such as the App version, not your account details.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and secure accounts;
  • provide Bible reading, saved content, plans, lessons, the Library, the Relationship Map, Support Circle, features you do with other people, Play, synchronization, and personalized suggestions;
  • provide Christian Living, Trusted Finds and ministry publications, review suggested and reported Finds, and remember your private saved items;
  • provide Moments, synchronize the relationship information you deliberately save, bring in the birthdays you tick, schedule the reminders you request, and return editable Relationship Assistant proposals;
  • generate and store Companion conversations;
  • send the notifications described in section 2.9 and keep the widgets described in section 2.11 current;
  • read and act on feedback, and fix the problems it reports;
  • detect possible crisis language and direct users toward human support;
  • investigate reported Companion responses, abuse, fraud, security incidents, and technical failures;
  • understand, in totals, which features and themes readers use, and maintain and improve reliability and safety; and
  • comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our agreement with you, your consent for optional features, permissions or profile information, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the App, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent for future optional processing, but that does not invalidate earlier lawful processing.

4. Service providers and disclosure

We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising or tracking. We disclose information only as described below.

Anthropic

We send Companion messages and the contextual fields described in section 2.3, and deliberately submitted Relationship Assistant text described in section 2.8, to Anthropic’s commercial API to generate responses or structured proposals. Under Anthropic’s published standard commercial API practices, inputs and outputs are not used for model training by default and are ordinarily deleted from its backend within 30 days. Anthropic may retain some data longer where required for law or usage-policy enforcement, including flagged content. Its current terms and our actual account settings control if they change.

Supabase

Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, private file storage, and backend functions. It processes account and app data on our behalf. We use encrypted connections, owner-scoped storage policies, and database row-level access controls.

Expo, Apple, and Google (updates and push notifications)

Expo delivers App updates and relays push notifications to Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging, which deliver them to your device. They receive the device push token and the notification's generic text, not your account details or anything you wrote.

Google and Apple sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, that service confirms your identity to us under its own privacy policy.

Sentry

Sentry processes the diagnostics described in section 2.12 to help us identify crashes, performance problems, and errors.

Bible text providers

Our Bible function requests a Bible version and chapter reference from YouVersion Platform, API.Bible, or bible-api.com to retrieve Scripture text and required attribution. These providers do not receive your App account identity from us, although their servers may receive ordinary request metadata from our backend.

Unsplash

The nature picture of the day is fetched by our server from Unsplash once a day for all readers, with the photographer's credit. Unsplash does not receive anything about you from us.

External retailers, venues, and experience providers

Trusted Finds open a clean direct retailer or provider URL in your browser. We do not append your account identity or any App data. The external destination may receive ordinary web-request information and handles its own payment, booking, delivery, refunds, cookies, and privacy practices. We receive nothing back from it.

Legal and safety disclosures

We may preserve or disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law or lawful process; protect the rights, security, and integrity of users, the public, the App, or our providers; investigate abuse; or respond to an emergency involving risk of death or serious physical harm. The App is not continuously monitored and is not an emergency service.

5. Crisis detection and response reporting

The App checks each Companion message for language that may indicate self-harm, abuse, or danger. A device-side check can intervene before a message is sent; the server repeats the check. If triggered, the normal input is hidden and Connect with Someone is shown. We use this only to change the in-app response and provide support options, not to diagnose you.

You can report a Companion response. The report links your account, selected reason, reported response, and the minimum conversation context required for a restricted manual review. Reports are not visible to other users.

6. Storage, security, and international transfers

Our providers may process information outside Uganda or your country. Those locations may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on contractual safeguards or other lawful transfer mechanisms provided by our service providers.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted transmission, protected authentication tokens, on-device encryption for Moments and queued offline writes, access controls, and database row-level security. No online service can promise absolute security. Please use a unique password and do not place information in Companion that you do not want processed as described here.

7. Retention and deletion

We generally keep account and app data while your account is active. You may permanently delete your account from Me → About → Delete account. Deletion removes your authentication account and associated profile, saved content, uploaded Expression images, journey, reading and plan data, Library positions and highlights, Christian Living records, Find suggestions and reports, Moments people (including imported birthdays), memories, situations and actions, Support Circle links, plan groups, together weeks, Closer games, gameplay data, push tokens, feedback and its screenshots, Companion conversations, and response reports from our active systems. It cannot be undone.

Deletion from our active systems may not immediately remove encrypted backups, security logs, or data already held temporarily by a provider. Those copies expire under provider retention schedules unless law or security needs require longer preservation. Anthropic’s standard API retention is described in section 4. Sentry controls diagnostic retention under our project configuration. We may retain limited records where legally required or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8. Your choices and rights

You can edit profile information; inspect, correct, archive or delete what Moments remembers; turn contacts, calendar, camera, photos and notification permissions on or off in your phone's settings; remove Support Circle connections; leave a plan group or stop playing Closer; remove widgets; choose whether to use optional Companion, Relationship Assistant, reminders and Play features; and delete your account in the App. Depending on your location, you may also request access, correction, a portable copy, restriction, objection, or deletion of personal information, and may complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact therelationshipbibleapp@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and must not be used by anyone below the minimum age required to consent to an online service in their country without legally valid parental authorization. If you believe a child has provided information contrary to this section, contact us and we will investigate and delete it where required.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the App or law changes. We will change the “Last updated” date and, where required, provide an in-app notice or request consent before a material change takes effect.
